A Closer Look

What makes Metemi structurally different

Not just a feature difference — a design philosophy built around one outcome: the real‑life meeting.

Real-Time Discovery

You see who's around you right now and actively open to meeting — a natural, contextual opportunity traditional apps don't offer.

Open Phrases

Built-in conversation starters remove the blank first-message anxiety and function as structured icebreakers that help both parties relax.

Individual & Group Meetings

Supports both one-on-one and group meeting formats — a meaningful choice for users who prefer a lower-stakes first encounter.

Distance, Not Location

Shows "0.4 km away" but never a map pin. Reduces friction while preserving a meaningful privacy boundary.

No Algorithmic Lock-In

Visibility isn't something you can buy or game — it's a function of where you are and when you choose to be discoverable.

Strict Safety Controls

Granular control over who can see you and when. Visibility toggles instantly — practical safety beyond standard privacy settings.
